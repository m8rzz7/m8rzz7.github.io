Welcome!

Thank you for checking out my website! I typed out every character to create this bad boy (No AI used!), and I hope you enjoy it. It is a current work in progress that recently (March 2026) started working on and updating. I initially created this website in Spring of 2024 while taking 355:425 Web Authoring at Rutgers University-New Brunswick, where I studied for my undergraduate degree in Computer Science.

If you're thinking to yourself, "Wow this site is so simple and boring. I bet ChatGPT could make a better one." I care about the environment and humanity. As someone who studied computer science and understands how AI actually works on a computational level, it is not this magic tool that lay men think it is. Quite literally, we are to running out of water and entering an era of water bankruptcy. And yes, Artificial Intelligence absolutely is a significant contributor to this global issue that will impact every single human being.

I love creativity. I love art. Artificial Intelligence can never create real art because it lacks soul, purpose, and intentionality. Anything any human makes, no matter how crappy it is (in my personal opinion) will always be infinitely better than the polluting slop computational generated by Artificial Intelligence. Keep humanity alive and start using your own brain to think creatively!
